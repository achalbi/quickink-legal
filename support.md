# QuickInk support

Email **admin@thoughtbasics.com** with any question or problem. Tell us which phone or browser you use and what you were doing when it went wrong. We usually reply within two working days.

QuickInk is made by **thoughtbasics** in Bengaluru, India.

## Common questions

### How do I sign in?

Use **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Google** on the sign-in screen. Your library is kept in your QuickInk account, so signing in with the same account on another phone or at [quickink.org](https://quickink.org) brings the same scans and notes.

### Why is something missing on my other phone?

Changes sync when both devices are online. Open QuickInk on the device that made the change and wait a moment with a connection. If it still does not appear, email us.

### What does "Online AI" do?

Online reading, titles and Ask send page text to our AI provider. QuickInk asks before it does this, and you can turn it on or off in **Settings › Privacy › Online AI**. Scanning, notes, search and the library work without it.

### I deleted something by mistake.

Deleted items wait in **Recently deleted** for 30 days. Open it from the Library and restore the item.

### How do I delete my account?

Open **Settings › Delete account**. This removes your account and all your data. See [Delete your QuickInk account and data](/delete-account/) for details, including how to ask by email if you cannot sign in.
